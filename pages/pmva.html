About Our PMVA Training

Our Prevention and Management of Violence and Aggression (PMVA) training programme equips healthcare professionals, support workers, carers and agency staff with practical techniques to safely manage challenging behaviour.

PMVA training focuses on prevention, proportionate lawful intervention only as a last resort, and protecting the safety, dignity and human rights of everyone involved. Training sessions combine theoretical learning, communication strategies, dynamic risk assessment and evidence‑based physical intervention techniques delivered by experienced instructors.
